## 14.611.1

* Trigger filters and setup
  * Added roll conditions for critical hits, advantage/disadvantage, natural 20/1, double natural 20/1 and maximum damage. Double naturals include any two matching dice from Elven Accuracy. Maximum damage also supports grouped formulas.
  * Compare rolled, calculated or applied damage/healing amounts and filter movement by distance. Applied amounts reflect actual HP and temporary HP changes, so *overkill* and excess healing do not inflate them.
  * Grouped HP, temporary HP and status filters under Actor state and Opponent state. Check required or excluded statuses and choose whether any or all required statuses must match.
  * Opponent Types can match subtypes, custom types and race identifiers. Use comma or semicolon-separated alternatives and partial matches such as `elf*` or `*red`.
  * Added **This item only** for source triggers. Limit a response to uses, attacks, damage, healing or target saves caused by its own item.
  * **Is Attacked** now has the same All / Hit / Miss filter as **Actor Attacks**. Grouped source and target events more clearly in the event list.
  * The trigger editor scrolls when needed while keeping Save and Cancel accessible. Improved filter spacing and moved explanatory hints to label tooltips.
  * Added **Ability Rolls** group.
    * Added **Roll Check**, **Check Succeeds**, **Check Fails** and **Roll Save** alongside **Save Succeeds** and **Save Fails** in a new **Ability Rolls** group.
    * Filter checks by ability, skill or tool. Roll Types distinguish checks, initiative, ordinary saves, death saves and concentration saves.
    * Roll Check and Roll Save work without a DC. Success/failure events require a recorded DC; initiative only fires Roll Check.
    * Fixed save triggers being skipped when a save message is first created, including responses to a failed save from the same item.
* Reactions and damage responses
  * Added **Counts as a reaction** to choose whether a triggered activity requires and spends a reaction.
  * **Is About to Be Hit** responses can automatically target the attacker.
  * Added **Actor Falls** for falling damage. It triggers after descent. With Mutable Damage Calculation enabled, damage protection responses can run during damage calculation before HP changes, even after the Falling status clears.
  * Added **Immune to Incoming Damage** for the current damage application. Protect selected damage types or All damage without a reduction formula or lasting actor changes.
  * Damage inclusion and exclusion lists now include the system's **Unique Types**, including Falling. New system-defined types become available automatically for filters and damage protection.
  * Spell triggers can offer an eligible higher-level slot when the use dialog is enabled. **Bypass use dialog** requires resources for the default casting configuration.
  * Fixed temporary-HP grants not firing Actor Heals or Is Healed.
* AC5e conditions
  * Use AC5e's condition editor alongside BM5e filters. Configured conditions appear green. When AC5e is unavailable, preserved conditions appear red with an option to remove them.
  * Conditions use recorded roll information, including advantage/disadvantage, mastery, ability modifiers and ammunition. Unsupported suggestions are disabled and missing data prevents conditions from passing accidentally.
  * Fixed standalone rolls incorrectly treating the rolling actor as an opponent. Closing the trigger editor while the AC5e editor is open no longer causes an error when returning.
* Chat cards
  * Merged overlapping refresh requests and skipped delayed updates for deleted messages or closed cards to reduce unnecessary rendering.
  * Fixed compact attack trays sometimes pinning chat to the bottom and preventing scrolling through older messages.
  * Attack button colors follow fumbles, recorded hit/miss results and AC changes from Shield-style responses.
  * Reaction offers expire without rebuilding cards. Deleting an attack cancels its pending reaction timers and closes its reaction dialogs.
  * Damage restoration and compact Apply controls avoid unnecessary rebuilding when token selection changes. Enchantment cards update selection only while visible.
  * Fixed a Foundry v14 compatibility warning when clearing the previous damage-button state.
* Token HUD and Active Effects
  * Moved status labels, sorting, search and scaling to **Bugbear's Assign Status Effects Sorter** module. A notice explains the move and saved HUD preferences remain available for import. BM5e keeps prone, grapple and falling mechanics.
  * Fixed a rider-item preparation loop on unlinked tokens when DAE is enabled. Rider items still follow their granting effect's active state.
* Compatibility and labels
  * Updated D&D5e compatibility verification to **6.1.1**.
  * Renamed bundled compendiums to **Items (BM5e)** and **Effects (BM5e)**, with consistent **(BM5e)** names for their entries.
  * Fixed the missing **Condition** label in Default Document Sheets.
